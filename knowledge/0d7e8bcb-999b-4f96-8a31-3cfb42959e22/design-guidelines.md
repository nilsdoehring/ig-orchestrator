## color_usage

- (100) Dark Blue (B7) is `#0b2a63`, used as the background behind the white logo.
- (100) Dark Midnight (B8) is `#001b41`, used for screen text.
- (100) Horizon (B3) is `#3196d6`, used for CTA buttons and text links.
- (100) IONOS Blue (B6) is `#003d8f`, used for the logo on light backgrounds and as the core brand colour.
- (100) Link hover (B4) is `#1474c4`, used for the link hover state and highlights on light backgrounds.
- (100) Never use off-palette hex codes, rainbow looks, effects, or tints that shift a colour, and never place a gradient over a photo.
- (100) Status colours are strictly functional: success green `#0fa954`, error red `#c80a00`, and warning yellow.
- (90) B5 is `#095bb1`, used for dark-background functional badges.
- (90) Blue Black (B9) is `#02102b`, used at the end of the Basic Blue gradient.
- (90) Cool Gray C2 is `#dbe2e8`, used for light-background functional badges.
- (90) Monochrome gradients must stay within one colour family and be at most three steps apart.
- (90) Sky is `#11c7e6`, used as a CTA accent and for highlights on dark backgrounds.
- (90) Stronger gradients may only use two colours from neighbouring families on the brand colour wheel, maximum three steps apart.
- (90) The Basic Blue gradient runs from B6 `#003d8f` to B9 `#02102b`, preferably at a 60° or -60° angle.
- (80) Secondary colours Amber `#ffaa00`, Rose `#ff6159`, Green `#12cf76`, Purple `#d746f5` need brand-contact approval and are used sparingly for spot badges and illustration.

## typography_scale

- (100) Body text line-height must be 125–150%, with 0 letter-spacing on body, looser on captions, and tighter on large headlines.
- (100) Never use all-caps in headlines or body copy; all-caps are reserved for scannable phrases of at most three words.
- (100) Text is always flush left; centring is an exception allowed only for CTA buttons and special headlines.
- (100) The base typography scale is body3, which equals 1rem or 16px on desktop, from which everything scales.
- (100) The body1 style is Open Sans Regular at 1.5rem, used for page leads.
- (100) The body2 style is Open Sans Regular at 1.25rem, used for section leads.
- (100) The body3 and body4 styles are Open Sans Regular at 1rem, used for long and short body copy.
- (100) The body5 style is Open Sans Regular at 0.8rem, used for large captions.
- (100) The body6 style is Open Sans Regular at 0.75rem, used for small captions like legal and metadata.
- (100) The brand uses two type families: Overpass for display and headlines, and Open Sans for titles, body, and button text.
- (100) The display1 style is Overpass Regular at 3rem, used for document headlines (stage).
- (100) The display2 style is Overpass Regular at 2.25rem, used for section headlines.
- (100) The display3 style is Overpass Semibold at 1.75rem, used for feature headlines on cards.
- (100) The display4 style is Overpass Regular at 2.5rem, used for document subheadlines.
- (100) The display5 style is Overpass Regular at 2.25rem, used for section subheadlines.
- (100) The display6 style is Overpass Semibold at 1.75rem, used for feature subheadlines.
- (100) The display9 style is Open Sans Semibold at 1rem, used for highlight badges.
- (100) The heading1 style is Open Sans Semibold at 1.5rem, used for document titles.
- (100) The heading2 style is Open Sans Semibold at 1.25rem, used for section titles.
- (100) The heading3 style is Open Sans Bold at 1rem, used for feature titles.
- (100) The heading4 and heading5 styles are Open Sans Bold at 0.75rem, used for paragraph titles, overlines, and caption titles.
- (100) The heading6 style is Open Sans Semibold at 1rem, used for CTA buttons.
- (90) Line length must be between 30 and 80 characters, avoiding widows and orphans.
- (90) Overpass Mono is used exclusively for code snippets.
- (90) The display8 style is Overpass Regular at 1.5rem, used for pull quotes.

## components

- (100) CTA buttons use Horizon `#3196d6` or Sky, with heading6 (Open Sans Semibold 1rem) text centred inside the button.
- (100) Functional or neutral badges use Cool Gray C2 on light backgrounds and B5 on dark backgrounds.
- (100) In price tags, the base unit X must be at least 8pt on web.
- (100) On dark backgrounds, checkmarks use a 15% white circle with a 100% white tick.
- (100) On light backgrounds, checkmarks use a 40% B3 circle with a 100% B6 tick.
- (100) On web, checkmarks are 24px marks aligned against 16px body text, typically in centre-aligned lists of 3–4 USPs.
- (100) Price badges (e.g., `Save 30%`, `Gratis`, `Sale`) must use the Amber colour.
- (100) Price tags use Open Sans Bold with base text at 2X, price at 8X, currency/decimals at 5X, and pre/postline at 2X.
- (100) Product icons are never isolated and must only be used as overlays.
- (100) Third-party marks must keep their own colours and never be recoloured to blue or grey.
- (100) Value badges (e.g., `New`, `Recommended`, `50% Faster`) must use the Purple colour.
- (90) Highlight badges must contain at most three words and be used sparingly.

## logo_and_brand_elements

- (100) In the backdrop, N1 is 100% Sky, N2 is 50% Horizon, N3 is 30% B5, and N4 is 15% Horizon at 20% opacity.
- (100) In the logo lockup, the suffix is set in Overpass at three quarters of the logo height, using original files only.
- (100) Never transform, outline, shadow, skew, or use a retired version of the logo, and never place it on a busy background.
- (100) The Nlement inside the static logo must never be recoloured.
- (100) The logo clear space must be wider than the letter "N".
- (100) The logo minimum height is 16px on screen, 12px in partner contexts, and 3.2mm in print.
- (100) The wordmark `IONOS` must always be set in capitals.
- (90) The Nlement celebrity backdrop is derived from the letter "N" and tilted at a 26° angle.
- (90) The backdrop uses three to five Nlements entering from the lower right corner.
- (90) The transition lockup is `IONOS by 1&1` for owned channels and DE outbound, and `IONOS` for international outbound.

## layout_and_responsiveness

- (90) Desktop and mobile layouts must be checked separately to ensure consistency between the two.
- (90) The design system uses a 16px desktop base and scales from it, with no fixed breakpoints named in the style guide.

## accessibility

- (100) Do not use gradient text, arbitrary hues, or coloured body copy, as this compromises functional colours and contrast.
- (100) Text contrast must meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards: at least 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text.
