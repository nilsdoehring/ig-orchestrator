## photographic_style

- (90) Use bright, natural, and varied lighting, strictly avoiding dark, moody, or overly stylized setups.
- (85) Set subjects in context-appropriate, lived-in real environments rather than sterile studio setups.
- (80) Ensure clear tonal contrast between the subject and the background.
- (80) Imagery should feel bold, confident, and British, conveying modern tech with punchy, dynamic energy.

## composition_and_framing

- (95) Keep the focal subject inside the central band, as images are rendered square and then center-cropped to standard aspect ratios.
- (90) For cutout images, shoot a single, centered subject with an unambiguous silhouette against a plain, high-contrast background.

## subject_and_diversity

- (90) Always encode character demographics positively and early in the prompt, never in the negative prompt.
- (85) Default ethnicity distribution is approximately 70% White/European, 20% Black/African descent, and 10% East or Southeast Asian.
- (80) Default character ages should range between the 30s and 50s, with gender varied by narrative context.
- (80) Match body shape to the subject's role, using stocky or solid builds for manual trades and average or lean builds for office workers.

## color_direction

- (90) Do not assume IONOS brand colors apply to Fasthosts, as its specific color palette is currently undocumented.
- (80) Avoid excessive color saturation in imagery to maintain a modern, punchy tech feel.

## visual_tokens

- (90) Fasthosts uses the proprietary typeface AntennaCond for both headings and body copy.

## misc

- (90) Always include standard negative prompts to prevent text, watermarks, UI chrome, logos, and dark or flat lighting.
