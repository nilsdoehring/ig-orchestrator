## photographic_style

- (90) Imagery should feature clean, professional Spanish business settings with cool neutral tones and lived-in, context-appropriate backgrounds.
- (90) Lighting must be bright, natural, and varied, strictly avoiding dark, moody, or overly stylized setups.

## composition_and_framing

- (90) Photoreal images require opaque, full-scene backgrounds, while cutouts need plain, high-contrast backgrounds for clean transparent edges.
- (80) Keep the focal subject inside the central band, as images are rendered square and then center-cropped to standard aspect ratios.

## subject_and_diversity

- (90) Always encode demographics positively early in the prompt, defaulting to 30s-50s age range and regionally plausible Spanish personas.
- (80) Default ethnicity distribution is roughly 70% White/European, 20% Black/African descent, and 10% East or Southeast Asian.
- (80) Match body shape to the subject's role, using stocky or muscular builds for manual trades and average or lean builds for office workers.

## visual_tokens

- (90) Use the proprietary typeface FS Blake for headings and Open Sans for body copy.

## misc

- (90) Standard negative prompts must exclude text, watermarks, UI chrome, logos, distorted anatomy, and dark or flat shadowless lighting.
