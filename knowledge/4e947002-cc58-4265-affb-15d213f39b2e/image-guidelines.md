## photographic_style

- (95) Use bright, optimistic, natural, and warm lighting, avoiding dark, moody, or artificially lit sterile studio setups.
- (90) Imagery must feature real, relatable people in natural, candid moments, avoiding staged, stiff, or overly dramatic stock-photo appearances.
- (85) Lighting must be soft, even, and diffused without harsh directional shadows, strong side-lighting, or dramatic chiaroscuro.

## composition_and_framing

- (90) For cutout images, shoot a single centered subject against a plain high-contrast background to ensure a clean silhouette for transparent PNGs.
- (90) Frame subjects strictly in frontal or half-profile views at eye level; never use pure side profiles or extreme high or low angles.
- (90) Keep the focal subject inside the central band with generous margins, as the service renders square and center-crops to standard ratios.
- (80) Backgrounds should be tidy, bright indoor settings (home, office, coworking) with soft bokeh, keeping the background clean and uncluttered.
- (75) For website imagery, keep the foreground completely unobstructed without blurry foreground objects.
- (70) In social media and ad imagery, moderate low- or high-angle perspectives and subtle depth-of-field bokeh are permitted for added dynamism.

## subject_and_diversity

- (100) Always encode ethnicity positively and early in the prompt, never in the negative prompt.
- (90) For Germany (default), represent ~80% White/Northern-European, ~10% Black, ~7% East/SE Asian, and ~3% South Asian subjects.
- (90) For Spain or Italy, represent ~65% Mediterranean/Latin-European, ~20% White, ~10% Black, and ~5% East/SE Asian subjects.
- (85) Default to subjects in their 30s–50s, using stocky or muscular builds for manual trades and average or lean builds for office settings.
- (85) Subjects must display open posture and genuine smiles or engaged focus; never use crossed arms, hunched shoulders, or forced grins.
- (80) Devices must be clearly visible, held naturally close to the body, and shown in active use rather than as hidden or purely decorative props.
- (80) Subjects should appear confident and relatable in genuine, lived-in work settings ranging from traditional craftsmen to digital nomads.
- (75) Subjects should wear casual to smart-casual solid or large-pattern clothing in warm neutrals and whites, avoiding busy micro-prints.

## color_direction

- (75) Do not use colors outside the brand palette, unapproved gradient combinations, or transparencies that distort colors.
