## photographic_style

- (90) Imagery must look professional, restrained, and businesslike, featuring authentic materials and real German business environments without excessive props.
- (90) Lighting must be bright, natural, and varied, explicitly avoiding dark, moody, or overly stylized setups.

## composition_and_framing

- (90) Because images render square and are center-cropped for landscape or portrait ratios, always keep the focal subject inside the surviving central band.
- (90) Cutout images must feature a single, centered subject with an unambiguous silhouette on a plain, high-contrast background for clean transparent PNG extraction.
- (80) Photoreal images require opaque, lived-in, context-appropriate backgrounds rather than sterile studio setups.

## subject_and_diversity

- (90) Explicitly encode demographics early in the prompt, defaulting to 70% White/European, 20% Black/African descent, and 10% East or Southeast Asian.
- (80) Subjects should default to their 30s–50s, using stocky or muscular builds for manual trades and average or lean builds for office roles.

## color_direction

- (90) Do not assume IONOS colors apply to UDAG; the specific primary and secondary color palettes are currently pending.

## visual_tokens

- (90) Both headings and body text use the Nunito Sans typeface, with Google Fonts as the fallback.

## misc

- (90) Always include a standard negative prompt blocking text, watermarks, UI chrome, readable screens, brand logos, and dark or flat lighting.
