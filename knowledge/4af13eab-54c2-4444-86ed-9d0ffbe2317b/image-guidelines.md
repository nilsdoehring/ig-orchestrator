## photographic_style

- (90) Imagery should depict real Polish small-business owners in friendly, human, and relatable settings.
- (90) Use bright, natural, and warm lighting, avoiding dark, moody, or overly stylized setups.
- (80) Backgrounds for photoreal images must be context-appropriate and lived-in, never sterile studio setups.

## composition_and_framing

- (90) Cutout images require a single centered subject with an unambiguous silhouette on a plain, high-contrast background.
- (90) Keep the focal subject inside the central band, as images are rendered square and then center-cropped to the target aspect ratio.

## subject_and_diversity

- (90) Always encode character demographics positively early in the prompt, using regionally plausible persona cues for the Polish market.
- (80) Default characters to their 30s–50s, varying body shape by role, such as stocky for manual trades and lean for office workers.

## visual_tokens

- (90) Both headings and body text use Azo Sans, a geometric sans-serif typeface.

## misc

- (100) Always use a negative prompt blocking text, watermarks, UI chrome, logos, distorted anatomy, oversaturation, and dark or flat lighting.
