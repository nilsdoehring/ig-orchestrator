## photographic_style

- (90) Feature real Austrian work settings with context-appropriate, lived-in backgrounds instead of sterile studio setups.
- (90) Images must feel bright, human, and optimistic, using natural, varied lighting rather than dark, moody, or overly stylized setups.

## composition_and_framing

- (90) Because images render square and are center-cropped to standard ratios, always keep the focal subject safely inside the surviving central band.
- (90) For cutout images, shoot a single centered subject against a plain high-contrast background to ensure a clean alpha edge for transparent PNGs.
- (90) Photoreal images must always feature an opaque, full-scene background.

## subject_and_diversity

- (100) Always encode character demographics positively and early in the prompt, never relying on the negative prompt for diversity.
- (80) Default ethnicity distribution should be approximately 70% White/European, 20% Black/African descent, and 10% East or Southeast Asian.
- (80) Feature subjects in their 30s to 50s, using stocky or muscular builds for manual trades and average or lean builds for office roles.

## visual_tokens

- (90) Use Satoshi for headings and Inter for body copy as the brand's primary typefaces.

## misc

- (90) Always use negative prompts to prevent text, watermarks, UI chrome, distorted anatomy, logos, and dark or flat lighting.
