## photographic_style

- (90) Use bright, natural, and varied lighting, avoiding dark, moody, or overly stylized setups.
- (80) Imagery must be practical, straightforward, and uncluttered, featuring real settings without excessive props or decoration.
- (80) Photoreal images require context-appropriate, lived-in, opaque backgrounds rather than sterile studio setups.

## composition_and_framing

- (90) Because images render square and center-crop to landscape or portrait, always keep the focal subject inside the surviving central band.
- (90) Cutout images must feature a single, centered subject with an unambiguous silhouette against a plain, high-contrast background.

## subject_and_diversity

- (90) Always encode character demographics positively and early in the prompt, never in the negative prompt.
- (80) Default to 30s-50s age range, applying regionally plausible persona cues for the Polish market.
- (70) Vary body shapes by role, using stocky or muscular builds for manual trades and average or lean builds for office roles.

## color_direction

- (90) Strefa has a distinct color palette; never assume or apply home.pl brand colors.

## visual_tokens

- (90) Use Montserrat (Google Fonts) for both headings and body text.

## misc

- (90) Always exclude text, watermarks, UI chrome, legible labels, brand logos, oversaturation, and dark moody or flat shadowless lighting.
